REDLINE VR

A tiny room-scale VR shooter prototype. Red targets move only when you move — stop, aim, and fire.

In headset: press Enter VR, allow the session, then use the right controller trigger to shoot. Move your head to advance time; stay still to slow the world.

Phone preview: swipe to aim and tap to shoot. In VR, aim the right controller and squeeze its trigger.

For headset mode, use Enter Quest 2 VR while viewing this page in the headset browser.

For headset VR, host this HTML on an HTTPS website, then open it in your headset browser.
